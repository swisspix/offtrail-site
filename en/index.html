Where to refill, where to sleep, how to get home.

Draw your route on the swisstopo national map: Offtrail snaps it onto real trails and shows you water, huts, shops and transport all along the way.

Download on the App Store

iPhone and iPad · no account · no ads

Draw your route

Press and hold the map, search for a place or import a GPX file: the route follows real trails, with distance, ascent and walking time.

Everything along the way

Water points, fountains, staffed and unstaffed huts, campsites, shelters, shops, buses and trains, kilometre by kilometre.

Stretches without water

More than 8 km with no water point? The stretch is flagged on the map, in the list and on the elevation profile.

Even with no signal

With Offtrail Plus, download the swisstopo map around your route, aerial photo included, and take it into the mountains.

Offtrail Plus

Drawing routes, searching along the way and the elevation profile are free. Offtrail Plus adds offline maps, GPX export and unlimited saved routes: CHF 3 a month, CHF 19 a year with a 7-day free trial, or CHF 49 once and for all.
