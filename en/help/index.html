Help

Answers to the most common questions. Something else? Write to contact@offtrail-app.ch.

Drawing a route

Press and hold the map to place the start, then the finish, and add stops if you like. You can also search for a place by name or import a GPX file. The route snaps onto the trails on its own; choose the preferred kind of way (trails, minor roads, roads, shortest) in Settings. To move a point, press and hold it and drag; tap it to delete it.

Finding water, huts and transport

Tap “Done”: Offtrail searches along the route, in a corridor you set from 100 m to 2 km. The categories searched are chosen in Settings. Huts with water carry a small blue drop on the map and “drinking water” or “water in season” in the list.

Where does the data come from?

Maps come from swisstopo, places from OpenStreetMap and refuges.info. A place is missing or wrong? You can fix it directly on openstreetmap.org or refuges.info: it will then show up in Offtrail. Always check on the spot, and check hut opening times before you set out.

What does the red zone mean?

A red zone on the map and on the elevation profile marks a stretch of more than 8 km with no water point found: carry enough water before you start it.

Using Offtrail with no signal

With Offtrail Plus, tap “Download offline map” under your route: the swisstopo map is saved 1.5 km either side of the route, with the aerial photo if you like. It then shows with no signal. Routing and search still need a connection. Downloaded maps are managed in Settings.

Offtrail Plus

Offtrail Plus unlocks offline maps, GPX export and unlimited saved routes (the free version keeps 3). Monthly (CHF 3) or annual subscription (CHF 19, 7-day free trial), or a one-time lifetime purchase (CHF 49). New device? Settings › Offtrail Plus › Restore purchases.

Managing or cancelling the subscription

In Offtrail: Settings › Offtrail Plus › Manage subscription. Or on the iPhone: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Saving and exporting

“Save” keeps the route and its places on your iPhone, in “My routes”. “Export” creates a GPX file with the route and places for a watch or GPS (Offtrail Plus).
