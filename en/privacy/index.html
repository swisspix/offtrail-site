Privacy

Updated: October 2026

Offtrail collects no personal data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

On your device

Saved routes and downloaded maps stay on your device only. Your location, if you allow it, is only used to show you on the map; it is neither stored nor sent anywhere.

Services used

To show the map, calculate routes and find places, the app queries public services directly: swisstopo and geo.admin.ch (maps, place search), BRouter (routing), Overpass / OpenStreetMap (places) and refuges.info (huts and water points). These requests contain the coordinates of the area concerned and, like any internet connection, your IP address. Offtrail has no server of its own and receives nothing.

Purchases

Offtrail Plus purchases go through Apple's App Store. Offtrail sees neither your identity nor your payment details.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. The host may log IP addresses to keep the service secure.
